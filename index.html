Satellite Intelligence

Seasonal Sentinel-2 spectral indicators for vegetation, surface water and vegetation moisture assessment.

SENTINEL-2
NDVI

Vegetation Index

Indicates relative vegetation greenness and canopy condition from red and near-infrared reflectance.

Raster source: combined indices TIFF
NDWI

Water Index

Spectral indicator for assessing water-related surface characteristics.

Raster source: combined indices TIFF
MNDWI

Modified Water Index

Uses green and shortwave infrared reflectance to enhance open-water feature detection.

Raster source: combined indices TIFF
NDMI

Moisture Index

Spectral indicator associated with vegetation canopy water content.

MODULE 03

Rainfall, Terrain & Hydrology

Terrain-derived drainage characteristics and seasonal rainfall context for watershed investigation.

Terrain and Drainage Analysis

SRTM / D8

Flow direction, flow accumulation and stream extraction are terrain-derived outputs, not surveyed stream measurements.

Hydrological Data Sources

Rainfall CHIRPS daily precipitation
Terrain SRTM elevation model
Flow method D8 flow direction
Drainage extraction Multiple flow accumulation thresholds
Resolution consideration

CHIRPS rainfall has a much coarser spatial resolution than SRTM. Resampling rainfall does not create finer-resolution rainfall information.

MODULE 05 OUTPUT

Priority Model

The project priority score combines four normalised spatial indicators:

WEIGHTED PRIORITY FORMULA 0.40 × Relative Stress
+ 0.25 × Moisture Stress
+ 0.20 × Slope Suitability
+ 0.15 × Drainage Suitability
Use of candidate locations

Candidate locations are proposed field-investigation points. They are not approved construction, groundwater recharge or engineering sites.
