MODULE 02

Satellite Indicators

Seasonal Sentinel-2 spectral indicators derived from the combined multiband GeoTIFF.

KHARIF 2025
Combined satellite dataset

The four indices are stored as bands in Bayad_Kharif_2025_Indices.tif. They are not separate TIFF files. Band values and raster maps require the original multiband raster or a connected backend.

NDVI

Vegetation Index

Normalized Difference Vegetation Index. Used to examine vegetation greenness and relative vegetation condition.

Band 1 · Combined TIFF
NDWI

Water Index

Normalized Difference Water Index. Provides a spectral response associated with water content and surface water.

Band 2 · Combined TIFF
MNDWI

Modified Water Index

Modified Normalized Difference Water Index. Helps distinguish water features from certain built-up and soil backgrounds.

Band 3 · Combined TIFF
NDMI

Moisture Index

Normalized Difference Moisture Index. Used as a relative indicator of vegetation moisture conditions.

Band 4 · Combined TIFF

Note: These spectral indices are proxies and do not directly measure crop yield, groundwater availability or field soil moisture.

MODULE 04

Agricultural Stress Assessment

Relative vegetation and moisture stress screening for the Kharif 2025 analysis.

GEOAI ANALYSIS

Stress Classification

Lower Relative Stress

Stress score below 0.25

Moderate Relative Stress

Stress score 0.25–0.50

Elevated Relative Stress

Stress score 0.50–0.75

Higher Relative Stress

Stress score 0.75 and above

Relative Stress Model 0.50 × NDVI Stress + 0.50 × NDMI Stress
