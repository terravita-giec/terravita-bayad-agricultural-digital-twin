Assessment Method

The pilot combines percentile-normalised NDVI and NDMI stress indicators over the vegetated-pixel subset.

Relative Agricultural Stress 0.50 × NDVI Stress + 0.50 × NDMI Stress

These classes are relative to the selected study period. They are not calibrated drought thresholds or direct measurements of crop yield.
