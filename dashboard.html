Project Overview

From Satellite Data to Agricultural Intelligence

The TerraVita Bayad Agricultural Digital Twin integrates earth observation, terrain and rainfall datasets, spatial analysis and exploratory machine learning to identify relative agricultural stress and support location-based intervention screening.

01

Study Area

Bayad Taluka, Aravalli District, Gujarat, India.

02

Satellite Monitoring

Sentinel-2 spectral indices for vegetation and moisture-related condition screening.

03

Spatial Intelligence

Terrain, rainfall, drainage and agricultural stress analysis.

04

Decision Support

Priority mapping and spatial intervention scenario comparison.

Technology Stack

Geospatial Technology

A multi-source geospatial workflow combining satellite earth observation, GIS, Python analytics and interactive visualization.

Raster processing, automation and spatial analytics.

GIS & Remote Sensing

GeoPandas, Rasterio, QGIS and geospatial data processing.

GeoAI

Interactive maps, charts and spatial decision-support views.

Technical Workflow

Six Project Modules

The project is organized into six connected modules, from administrative boundary preparation to dashboard delivery.

01

AOI Verification

Administrative boundary preparation and spatial quality checks.

02

Satellite Indices

Sentinel-2 NDVI, NDWI, MNDWI and NDMI analysis.

03

Rainfall & Terrain

CHIRPS rainfall, SRTM elevation, slope and terrain-derived drainage.

04

Agricultural Stress & GeoAI

Relative stress screening and KMeans clustering.

05

Digital Twin Scenarios

Intervention priority scoring and buffer coverage comparisons.

06

Interactive Dashboard

GIS visualization, analytics and report downloads.
