Scientific Interpretation & Limitations

This project is a geospatial screening and decision-support prototype. Satellite vegetation and moisture indices are indirect indicators and do not independently establish crop yield, groundwater availability or agricultural drought. Exploratory GeoAI clusters are not ground-truth classifications. Intervention priority scores are preliminary assumptions and require field validation, hydrogeological assessment and stakeholder consultation before implementation.
